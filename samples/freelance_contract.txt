Independent Contractor Agreement (Sample - Brightloop Studio)

This Agreement is made between Brightloop Studio Inc. ("Company") and the individual signing below ("Contractor"), effective on the date of signature.

1. Services
Contractor will design and develop a marketing website and related assets as described in Statement of Work #1 ("Services"). Company may change the scope of the Services at any time by email, and Contractor shall perform such changes without additional compensation unless Company agrees otherwise in writing.

2. Fees and Payment
Company will pay Contractor a fixed fee of $8,000. Invoices are payable net ninety (90) days after Company's approval of the final deliverables. Company may withhold payment for any deliverable it determines, in its sole discretion, to be unsatisfactory.

3. Expenses
Contractor is responsible for all of its own expenses, including software licences, stock assets and hosting, unless pre-approved in writing.

4. Intellectual Property
Contractor hereby irrevocably assigns to Company all right, title and interest in any work product, inventions, code, designs and ideas created by Contractor during the term of this Agreement, whether or not related to the Services and whether or not created using Company resources. Contractor waives all moral rights.

5. Portfolio Use
Contractor may not display, reference or describe the work in any portfolio, website or social media without Company's prior written consent.

6. Confidentiality
Contractor shall keep confidential all non-public information of Company for a period of ten (10) years after termination.

7. Non-Competition and Non-Solicitation
For twenty-four (24) months after termination, Contractor shall not provide design or development services to any business that competes with Company or any of Company's clients anywhere in North America, and shall not solicit any Company client.

8. Termination
Company may terminate this Agreement at any time for any reason upon written notice. Upon termination, Company shall pay only for deliverables already approved. Contractor may terminate only for Company's material breach that remains uncured for sixty (60) days.

9. Indemnification
Contractor shall defend, indemnify and hold harmless Company and its clients against any claims, losses or expenses arising out of or related to the Services, including claims caused in part by Company.

10. Limitation of Liability
In no event shall Company be liable to Contractor for any amount exceeding the fees actually paid in the three (3) months before the claim.

11. Independent Contractor Status
Contractor is an independent contractor and is responsible for all taxes. Contractor shall be available during Company's business hours and attend daily stand-up meetings.

12. Governing Law and Venue
This Agreement is governed by the laws of Delaware. The exclusive venue for any dispute shall be the courts of Wilmington, Delaware.

13. Entire Agreement
This Agreement is the entire agreement between the parties. Company may update this Agreement by posting a revised version, and Contractor's continued performance constitutes acceptance.
