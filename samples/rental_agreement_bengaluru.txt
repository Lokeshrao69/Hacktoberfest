Rental Agreement (Sample - Flat 304, Shanti Residency, Bengaluru)

This Rental Agreement is executed at Bengaluru on 1st March 2026 between Sri Lakshmi Estates, represented by its proprietor (hereinafter "the Owner"), and the undersigned tenant (hereinafter "the Tenant"), for residential Flat No. 304, Shanti Residency, 14th Main, HSR Layout, Bengaluru - 560102 (hereinafter "the Premises"). This sample is fictional and for demonstration only.

1. TERM AND RENEWAL
The tenancy shall be for a period of eleven (11) months commencing from 1st April 2026. The agreement may be renewed only at the option of the Owner, and upon every renewal the monthly rent shall be increased by a minimum of ten percent (10%) or such higher amount as the Owner may decide.

2. RENT AND MAINTENANCE
The Tenant shall pay a monthly rent of Rs. 32,000 (Rupees Thirty-Two Thousand only) on or before the 1st day of every English calendar month, in cash or by bank transfer. Apartment maintenance charges of Rs. 3,500 per month shall be paid separately by the Tenant to the Owner.

3. LATE PAYMENT
If the rent is not paid by the 1st of the month, the Tenant shall pay a penalty of Rs. 500 per day of delay. If the delay exceeds fifteen (15) days, the Owner shall be entitled to disconnect the electricity and water supply to the Premises and to lock the Premises until all dues are cleared.

4. SECURITY DEPOSIT
The Tenant has paid an interest-free security deposit of Rs. 3,20,000 (Rupees Three Lakh Twenty Thousand only), equal to ten (10) months' rent. The deposit shall be refunded within sixty (60) days after the Tenant vacates, after deducting one (1) month's rent towards painting charges irrespective of the condition of the Premises, deep-cleaning charges, and any other amount the Owner deems fit.

5. LOCK-IN PERIOD
The Tenant agrees to a lock-in period of eleven (11) months. If the Tenant vacates the Premises for any reason before the expiry of the lock-in period, the entire security deposit shall stand forfeited. The Owner may terminate this agreement at any time by giving fifteen (15) days' notice.

6. NOTICE PERIOD
After the lock-in period, the Tenant shall give three (3) months' written notice before vacating, or pay three months' rent in lieu of notice.

7. REPAIRS AND MAINTENANCE
The Tenant shall carry out all repairs to the Premises at the Tenant's own cost, including structural repairs, seepage and waterproofing, plumbing, electrical wiring and repairs to fittings and fixtures provided by the Owner.

8. INSPECTION
The Owner or any person authorised by the Owner may enter and inspect the Premises at any time, with or without notice to the Tenant.

9. USE OF PREMISES
The Premises shall be used only for residential purposes by the Tenant and the Tenant's immediate family. No guests shall stay overnight without the Owner's prior permission. The Tenant shall not cook non-vegetarian food on the Premises and shall not keep pets.

10. ELECTRICITY AND WATER
Electricity shall be billed through a sub-meter installed by the Owner at the rate of Rs. 12 per unit, irrespective of the BESCOM tariff. Water charges of Rs. 800 per month shall be payable in addition to maintenance.

11. STAMP DUTY AND REGISTRATION
The entire stamp duty, notary charges and any registration charges for this agreement shall be borne solely by the Tenant. The parties agree that this agreement need not be registered.

12. CHANGES TO TERMS
The Owner may revise the rent, maintenance charges or any terms of this agreement by informing the Tenant over WhatsApp or SMS. Continued occupation of the Premises by the Tenant shall amount to acceptance of the revised terms.

13. INDEMNITY
The Tenant shall indemnify and keep the Owner harmless against all losses, claims, damages and legal costs of any nature arising out of the occupation of the Premises, including those caused by defects in the building.

14. DISPUTE RESOLUTION
Any dispute arising out of this agreement shall be referred to a sole arbitrator appointed by the Owner. The seat of arbitration shall be Chennai and the proceedings shall be in English. The arbitrator's fees shall be borne by the Tenant.

15. POLICE VERIFICATION
The Tenant shall provide copies of identity documents to the Owner for tenant police verification as required by local authorities.

16. JURISDICTION
This agreement shall be governed by the laws of India and the courts at Bengaluru shall have jurisdiction.
