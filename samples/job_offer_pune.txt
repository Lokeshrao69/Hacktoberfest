Offer of Employment (Sample - Nimbus Softech Pvt. Ltd., Pune)

Dear Candidate, we are pleased to offer you the position of Software Engineer at Nimbus Softech Private Limited ("the Company") at our Hinjewadi, Pune office on the following terms. This sample is fictional and for demonstration only.

1. Compensation
Your annual Cost to Company (CTC) will be Rs. 12,00,000 (Rupees Twelve Lakh only), which includes a variable pay component of Rs. 2,00,000 payable at the sole discretion of the management, and the employer's contribution to Provident Fund and gratuity. A joining bonus of Rs. 50,000 will be paid with your first salary and shall be fully recoverable if you leave the Company within twenty-four (24) months.

2. Probation
You will be on probation for six (6) months, which may be extended at the Company's discretion. During probation the Company may terminate your employment without notice or pay, while you are required to give thirty (30) days' notice.

3. Service Bond
You agree to serve the Company for a minimum period of three (3) years from the date of joining. If you resign or are terminated for any reason before completing three years, you shall pay the Company Rs. 3,00,000 as liquidated damages, irrespective of the training actually provided. Your original educational certificates shall be retained by the Company until the bond period is completed.

4. Notice Period
After confirmation, you must give ninety (90) days' notice of resignation. Notice period buyout is at the sole discretion of the Company, and earned leave cannot be adjusted against the notice period. The Company may terminate your employment by giving thirty (30) days' notice or salary in lieu thereof.

5. Working Hours
Your normal working hours will be nine (9) hours per day, five days a week. You may be required to work on weekends and public holidays as per project needs, without overtime pay or compensatory off.

6. Deductions
The Company may deduct from your salary or full and final settlement any amount that the Company considers due from you, including notice shortfall, bond amount, unreturned assets and any losses caused to the Company.

7. Intellectual Property
All inventions, code, designs and works created by you during your employment, whether or not during working hours and whether or not using Company equipment, including any inventions you created before joining, shall be the exclusive property of the Company, and you hereby assign all rights in them to the Company.

8. Non-Compete
For a period of two (2) years after leaving the Company, you shall not take up employment with any competitor of the Company or with any client of the Company anywhere in India.

9. Non-Solicitation
For twelve (12) months after leaving, you shall not solicit any employee of the Company to leave the Company.

10. Confidentiality
You shall keep all confidential information of the Company and its clients strictly confidential during and after your employment.

11. Monitoring and Personal Data
The Company may monitor your official and personal devices, email and social media accounts used for work. The Company may share your personal data, including Aadhaar and PAN details, with its affiliates and third parties for any business purpose.

12. Transfer
You may be transferred to any office, client site or group company in India or abroad at the sole discretion of the Company.

13. Leave
You will be entitled to eighteen (18) days of paid leave per calendar year. Unused leave will lapse at the end of each year and cannot be carried forward or encashed.

14. Changes to Policies
The Company may amend any of these terms or its HR policies at any time by publishing the revised policy on the intranet, and such changes shall be binding on you.

15. Dispute Resolution
Any dispute shall be resolved by a sole arbitrator appointed by the Company's Managing Director. The seat of arbitration shall be Pune and the courts at Pune shall have exclusive jurisdiction.
