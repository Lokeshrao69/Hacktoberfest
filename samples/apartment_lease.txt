Residential Lease Agreement (Sample - Maple Court Apartments)

This Residential Lease Agreement is entered into on March 1, 2026 between Maple Court Holdings LLC ("Landlord") and the undersigned resident ("Tenant") for Unit 4B, 220 Birch Street.

1. TERM
The lease begins on April 1, 2026 and ends on March 31, 2027. This lease shall automatically renew for successive twelve (12) month terms unless Tenant delivers written notice of non-renewal no fewer than ninety (90) days before the end of the then-current term. Rent for any renewal term may be increased at Landlord's sole discretion.

2. RENT
Monthly rent is $2,150, due on the first (1st) day of each month. Rent must be paid through Landlord's online portal; a non-refundable processing fee of $9.95 applies to each payment.

3. LATE CHARGES
If rent is not received by 11:59 p.m. on the first day of the month, Tenant shall pay a late fee of $100 plus $25 per day until rent is paid in full. Late fees are considered additional rent.

4. SECURITY DEPOSIT
Tenant shall pay a security deposit of $4,300. Landlord may deduct from the deposit any amounts Landlord deems appropriate, including professional carpet cleaning and repainting regardless of condition. The balance, if any, will be returned within sixty (60) days after Tenant vacates.

5. UTILITIES
Tenant is responsible for electricity, internet and gas. Water, sewer and trash are billed by Landlord as a monthly flat fee of $85, subject to change upon notice.

6. MAINTENANCE AND REPAIRS
Tenant shall keep the unit clean and sanitary. Tenant shall be responsible for all repairs under $500, including repairs to appliances, plumbing fixtures and the heating system, at Tenant's sole expense. Tenant shall report water leaks within 24 hours.

7. ENTRY BY LANDLORD
Landlord and its agents may enter the premises at any time, with or without notice, to inspect, make repairs, or show the unit to prospective tenants or buyers.

8. USE AND OCCUPANCY
The unit shall be used solely as a private residence for Tenant and persons listed on the application. Guests staying more than seven (7) consecutive nights require Landlord's written approval.

9. PETS
No pets are allowed without a signed pet addendum. Unauthorized pets will result in a fee of $500 per occurrence.

10. EARLY TERMINATION
If Tenant vacates before the end of the term for any reason, all remaining rent for the entire term becomes immediately due and payable, together with an early termination fee equal to two (2) months' rent. Landlord has no obligation to re-let the premises.

11. RULES AND CHANGES
Landlord reserves the right to modify the community rules and the terms of this Lease at any time without prior notice. Continued occupancy constitutes acceptance of any changes.

12. LIABILITY
Landlord shall not be liable for any damage or injury to Tenant, guests, or property arising from any cause whatsoever, including Landlord's negligence. Tenant shall indemnify and hold harmless Landlord from any and all claims arising from Tenant's occupancy.

13. DISPUTE RESOLUTION
Any dispute arising under this Lease shall be resolved by binding arbitration administered by an arbitrator selected by Landlord. Tenant waives any right to a jury trial and to participate in a class action. Arbitration costs shall be split equally.

14. RENTER'S INSURANCE
Tenant shall maintain renter's insurance with liability coverage of at least $100,000 and name Landlord as an additional insured.

15. NOTICES
Notices to Tenant may be delivered by email or posted on the unit door. Notices to Landlord must be sent by certified mail to the management office.

16. GOVERNING LAW
This Lease is governed by the laws of the State in which the premises are located.
