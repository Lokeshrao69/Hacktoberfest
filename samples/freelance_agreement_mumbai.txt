Freelance Services Agreement (Sample - Pixelkart Digital LLP, Mumbai)

This Agreement is entered into at Mumbai between Pixelkart Digital LLP ("the Client") and the individual freelancer signing below ("the Freelancer"), effective from the date of signature. This sample is fictional and for demonstration only.

1. Scope of Work
The Freelancer shall design a mobile app UI and marketing website for the Client as described in Annexure A. The Client may add to or change the scope at any time by email, and the Freelancer shall incorporate such changes and unlimited revisions without any additional fee.

2. Fees
The Client shall pay a fixed fee of Rs. 1,50,000 (Rupees One Lakh Fifty Thousand only), inclusive of GST, for the entire project. No advance shall be paid.

3. Payment Terms
Invoices shall be payable within ninety (90) days after the Client's final approval of all deliverables. The Client may withhold payment for any deliverable that it considers unsatisfactory in its sole discretion. TDS shall be deducted as applicable.

4. Expenses
The Freelancer shall bear all expenses, including software subscriptions, stock images, fonts and travel to the Client's office in Andheri, Mumbai.

5. Intellectual Property
All work product, including drafts, rejected concepts, source files and any pre-existing templates or tools used by the Freelancer, shall become the exclusive property of the Client from the moment of creation, whether or not the fees have been paid. The Freelancer waives all moral rights.

6. Portfolio
The Freelancer shall not display or mention the work in any portfolio, Behance, Dribbble, LinkedIn or other social media without the Client's prior written consent.

7. Exclusivity and Non-Compete
During the project and for eighteen (18) months after its completion, the Freelancer shall not provide design services to any other company in the e-commerce or fintech sectors anywhere in India.

8. Termination
The Client may terminate this Agreement at any time for convenience by written notice and shall pay only for deliverables already approved. The Freelancer may terminate only if the Client commits a material breach that remains uncured for sixty (60) days.

9. Indemnity
The Freelancer shall indemnify the Client against all claims, losses and legal costs arising out of or relating to the work, including claims arising from content or instructions provided by the Client.

10. Limitation of Liability
In no event shall the Client be liable to the Freelancer for any amount exceeding Rs. 10,000.

11. Confidentiality
The Freelancer shall keep all information about the Client's business confidential for a period of five (5) years after completion.

12. Dispute Resolution
Any dispute shall be referred to a sole arbitrator nominated by the Client. The seat of arbitration shall be Mumbai.

13. Entire Agreement
This Agreement constitutes the entire agreement between the parties. The Client may update these terms by email, and the Freelancer's continued work shall constitute acceptance.
